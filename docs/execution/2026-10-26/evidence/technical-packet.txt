PART A — TECHNICAL PACKET

1. Scope and conventions

1.1 Exact domains. Trusted computations are over \mathbb Z, \mathbb Z/M\mathbb Z for M\ge 2, and normalized rationals. No IEEE-754 value is a trusted transition. Display rounding happens once, at egress, with the discarded remainder recorded.

1.2 Vocabulary.

Term Meaning
residue r_M(X) X \bmod M, the coordinate in the fundamental domain
lift K_M(X) \lfloor X/M\rfloor, the sheet coordinate
anchor observation \kappa_A = (r_A - r_M)M^{-1} \bmod A = K_M \bmod A
capacity certificate a proof that 0 \le K < A (single anchor) or K = Q C_s + \hat K with epoch Q retained
resolver status K_RESIDUE_ONLY | K_EXACT_BOUNDED | K_EPOCH_QUALIFIED | K_INVALID
certified bound the analytic maximum of \lvert K\rvert for a given parameter tuple and operation

1.3 Claim register. Every statement carries one of: PROVEN (exact identity or Lean-discharged), MEASURED (exact-integer test, range stated), CONDITIONAL (true under stated hypotheses), OPEN, RETIRED.

2. The load-bearing core

2.1 Capacity-alias theorem — PROVEN (elementary)

For every integer X = r_M + M K and every anchor A with \gcd(M,A)=1,
X' = X + MA = r_M + M(K+A)
satisfies r_M(X') = r_M(X) and r_A(X') = r_A(X).

Corollary 2.1.1. Any method using only (r_M, r_A) returns identical output for K and K+A. It determines at most K \bmod A.

Corollary 2.1.2. Dropping K_j without a certificate is the representative choice K_j = 0. It is exact only when K_j is proven zero.

This is the whole of the arithmetic argument. Every subsequent failure in the corpus is an instance of Corollary 2.1.2.

2.2 K-Elimination soundness — PROVEN (Lean 4, 0 sorry on core path)

For \gcd(M,A)=1 and 0 \le X < MA:
\kappa_A = (r_A - r_M)\,M^{-1} \bmod A = K_M(X).

On the "60-year problem" framing — RETIRED. The formula is one Garner mixed-radix digit. The architectural value is that each anchor read is lane-independent and fixed-work; the mathematical content is classical. Claim surfaces must say "architectural inversion of Garner" not "solution to the 60-year problem."

2.3 The q_{\text{small}} = t identity — PROVEN

When the modulus-switch target is the plaintext modulus t, the switch step is identical to BFV decryption rounding. The polynomial-evaluation stage of classical bootstrapping is eliminated.

This establishes: the depth-1 refresh shape is correct.
This does not establish: the carry K_j is correctly propagated. See §3.

2.4 The 4-prime re-cut — MEASURED

Profile Old headroom New headroom Required Outcome
secure_128 (3→4 primes) 42 bits 71 bits 47 bits simple refresh preserved; first post-refresh multiply correct

This is the proof that the capacity discipline pays. Headroom is necessary, not sufficient. The 4-prime fix removes one failure mode; it does not make public refresh correct.

2.5 The Hidden Carry identity — PROVEN

For N = MK + r with M \equiv -1 \pmod A:
v_A := N \bmod A = (r - K) \bmod A.

Verified exactly for all N \in [0, 5000) — zero mismatches. Closed-shell descent reads v_{37} = 36, 35, 34, 33 at N = 36, 72, 108, 144.

This is the identity that explains the failures. A dropped K is invisible in the residue r and visible as a sign flip in the anchor.

Controls that localize the failure:

3.3 Blanket centering — REJECTED by measurement

A production candidate that centers ciphertext coefficients into [-\tfrac{Q}{2}, \tfrac{Q}{2}] before tensor multiplication at both symmetric and public multiply boundaries was tested:

· Fixed the deterministic depth-4 witness (65{,}536 returned).
· Regressed 13 optimized tests: secure_128 repeated-square failed at depth 3 (250 vs 256); secure_192 failed at depth 4 (64,505 vs 65,536); public/symmetric identity checks failed.
· Rolled back. Patch preserved in centered_lift_candidate.patch.

Why it fails as a general fix. Centering selects K_Q \in \{0, -1\} by the sign of the residue. That is a data-dependent section, not a certificate. It cannot recover a lift the pipeline never observed. The shadow path worked because its inputs were reconstructed from the main CRT basis (the correct ciphertext state), not from the pipeline's corrupted intermediate.

This is a permanent rejection. Do not merge.

3.4 The "valid prior implementation" — IDENTIFIED

The working carry-handling logic lives in crates/ns_v5/recumbent.rs, developed for the Navier–Stokes solver. The FHE port (NINE65_v7) reinvented the logic via to_int / extract_k_rns / canonicalize_dual_anchor and lost the structural invariant that made the ns_v5 version correct. The Recumbent bootstrap .txt blueprint ports it back.

4. The lift contract

A correct public refresh requires a per-operation lift contract: for every coefficient entering the tensor product and every coefficient exiting the rescale, which representative is used, under what certificate.

4.1 Fresh-encryption lift. Phase is \Delta m + e with \lVert e\rVert bounded by parameters. The lift is bounded by a parameter property, not a per-ciphertext measurement. Certificate: EXACT_BOUNDED with the parameter bound.

4.2 Multiply-generated lift. After tensor and rescale, the lift is the sum of contributions from operands and secret-polynomial convolution. Magnitude is not bounded by encryption parameters. Certificate: either an epoch (carried) or an EXACT_BOUNDED reconstructed from the anchor at the multiply boundary.

4.3 The prohibited move. Centering intermediate tensor terms after their main-Q product has wrapped is a section that ignores provenance. Prohibited regardless of whether it fixes a witness.

4.4 The required move. Reconstruct K_Q from the anchor at every multiply input, verify 0 \le \lvert K_Q\rvert < A, reconstruct the centered representative from (r_Q, K_Q), and re-encode the authentic residues of the quotient into the anchor lanes at the rescale output. This is the recumbent port's BoundedResidueDivider::divide.

5. The Safe Basis construction

The current anchor track has no parity witness, no shadow anchor, and no phase lock. Commit 76435a4 flagged this. The blueprint's §6 constructs the missing structure.

5.1 Main lanes (star-family, free inverse)

Each q_i = c_i t + 1. Properties: q_i \equiv 1 \pmod t (message-transparent); t^{-1} \bmod q_i = q_i - c_i (construction-read; no extended GCD at runtime).

5.2 Primary anchor (adjacency, free inverse)

Each a_j = p_j + 1 with p_j prime. Properties: p_j^2 \equiv 1 \pmod{a_j}; p_j^{-1} \equiv p_j \pmod{a_j}. K-Elimination for divisor p_j is one modular subtraction.

5.3 Shadow anchor (paired, cross-check)

Each a'_j = p_j - 1. Combined with a_j: the pair (r_{a'_j}, r_{a_j}) encodes (\gamma + K, \gamma - K) and cross-checks K.

5.4 Parity witness (syndrome)

One additional prime w coprime to all main and anchor moduli. Forward-direction syndrome detection (Theorem T5, forward only): if X \equiv Y \pmod{m_i} and X \equiv Y \pmod w, then \gcd(m_i, w) \mid (X-Y).

5.5 What this buys

Property Without Safe Basis With Safe Basis
derive_winding cost one ext-GCD per anchor lane per coefficient zero (construction-read)
Message-transparent switch requires scaling factor free (q_i \equiv 1 \bmod t)
Shadow cross-check none paired anchors
Syndrome detection none parity witness

Phase 3 of the execution plan is not optional. It is the construction that makes Phase 2 fast and correct.

8. Claim surface

What can be said publicly today:

NINE65 v7 is an exact-integer BFV/DualRNS prototype with a working K-Elimination substrate, a fail-closed public-refresh gate, and a measured depth-4 carry-displacement bug localized to the unsigned auxiliary anchor lift. A recumbent port from the ns_v5 solver is the specified fix. Public refresh is disabled on all profiles until the recumbent rescale and bootstrap pass their gates. secure_256 is a target label with a 240-bit MATZOV binding, not a certified 256-bit profile.

What cannot be said:

· Unlimited depth
· Bootstrap-free
· Production-ready
· 256-bit secure under all models
· Constant-time across the library
· "One subtraction reconstructs every integer"
· "Residue-native everywhere" (bootstrap paths still reconstruct)

20. The one-paragraph summary

The depth-4 −4 and the step-32 −2 are the same event: a lift K_j chosen without a certificate, then divided exactly by \Delta. The Hidden Carry identity v_A = (r - K) \bmod A is the observation that recovers K_j exactly. The recumbent port from ns_v5/recumbent.rs is the working implementation of that observation, lost in the FHE rewrite. The critical path is: lock the baseline, port the recumbent types and BoundedResidueDivider, replace the rescale and bootstrap with the recumbent versions, install the Safe Basis construction (star-family main + adjacency anchors + shadow + parity witness) so the free inverses are construction-read, and fail closed on RNS products. Blanket centering is rejected by measurement. Route B (exact prime-drop) is a fallback if the recumbent pipeline cannot pass its gates. Public refresh stays disabled until Phase 2 + Phase 3 are green on all four profiles. Everything else — security attestation, performance parity, constant time — is a separate program with its own evidence.

That is the ground truth. It is narrow, it is measurable, and every claim in it maps to a named artifact or a named gate.
