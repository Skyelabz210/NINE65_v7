- Instrument an isolated off-path research probe for the actual shadow operations; record operation/lane/twiddle/source classification, seed provenance and sampling protocol.
- Test fixed public inputs, attacker-chosen inputs, actual ciphertext workloads and independently randomized private inputs as distinct populations.
- Export synthetic/non-secret trace datasets for the historical NIST harness and an independent statistical implementation; do not export production keys, secret-dependent raw shadows or user plaintexts.

## Acceptance criteria

- Twiddle-one, zero-input, repeat-input and cross-lane correlations are explicitly measured.
- Turning capture on/off produces bit-identical primary arithmetic outputs.
