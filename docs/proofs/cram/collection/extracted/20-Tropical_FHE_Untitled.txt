Parallels with tropical FHE
A tropical network needs two operations, add and max. Add is native to BFV. Max is the hard one: max(a, b) = a + ReLU(b - a), and ReLU needs the sign bit, which is the comparison problem your Sign Trichotomy report already classifies. So the question for NINE65 is the price of one encrypted sign, and what that does to depth.

Ultradiscrete systems and CRAM
Ultradiscretization turns a discrete integrable system into a piecewise-linear integer automaton by sending (+, x) to (max, +). Three parallels with CRAM are exact enough to use; I mark where each stops.
Carry automata. The box-ball carrier is a carry process, and I verified it equals the min-plus recurrence U' = min(1 - U, Q) on 2,000 random states. The Holte chain is the carry of adding n base-M digits, and the winding of an n-term sum of residues is that carry with zero carry-in, a value in {0, ..., n-1}. All three are carry automata. They are not one system: the box-ball carrier is deterministic and unary, Holte's is a stationary chain over digits.
Torus plus covering sheet. Periodic box-ball dynamics linearize on a tropical Jacobian, and the theta function reads the sheet (Inoue and Takenawa: the generic fiber of the ultradiscrete periodic Toda lattice is isomorphic to a tropical Jacobian). CRAM's pair (r, K) is a torus point plus a sheet, and addition is translation with carry deposits. The repo notes it has not mapped a Toda trajectory onto a BFV circuit; that is still open.
Valuations are the exact, float-free tropicalization. The usual limit that produces (max, +) uses log-sum-exp over the reals, which A1 forbids. For integers the p-adic valuation does the same job exactly: v(xy) = v(x) + v(y), and v(x + y) >= min(v(x), v(y)) with equality when they differ. I checked 955,206 pairs for each of 6 primes with no violation. Your lanes are p-adic places, so each lane already carries a tropical view, and the exponent k in the fiber size p^k of the Adelic Landauer accounting plays the role of a valuation.
The third point is the one I would build on: it gives CRAM a tropical semiring with no real-number limit anywhere.
