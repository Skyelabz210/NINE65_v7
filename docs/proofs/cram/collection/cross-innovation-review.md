## Results checked independently

**Consumer-specific anchors.** If existing source observations have period P and the requested target moduli have LCM D, adding an authentic observation modulo a gives period lcm(P,a). The least sufficient a under divisibility is the product of p raised to v_p(D) over primes for which v_p(D)>v_p(P). This follows prime by prime from the maximum exponent in an LCM. The tempting alternative D/gcd(D,P) can be too small because it supplies only the missing exponent difference rather than the entire required prime power. The map's P=12,D=18 example is correct: 3 does not suffice and 9 does. This is a written planning theorem; it does not create the new observation or implement a fused decoder.

**StarLift and the theta minimizer.** In the centered chart z=g+QK, Q>0 and |g|<Q/2, substitution n=j−K proves that the unique theta minimizer is −K. Integer adjacent-anchor observations of the actual reference g therefore give a residue of this minimizing index. The original canonical residue z mod Q may differ from g and yield a different quotient. The revised map explicitly requires integer Q,g,z and readings g mod A,z mod A for its ordinary integer StarLift instance, with A=Q+1; it identifies the reference correction and separate real-chart observation obligation. A public signed window is needed to select the integer minimizing index from its class. Theta walls retain their tie qualification.

**Local G/H lifting.** The existing [PublicRefreshDigit.lean](proof-check/native-refresh-source/KElimination/PublicRefreshDigit.lean) proves the polynomial Taylor remainder, exact division, canonical p² reduction and coherent H evaluation. Its premises are p>0, G(ρ)=0 modulo p², and G'(ρ)H(ρ)=1 modulo p. They imply that the protected divided defect is G'(ρ)K modulo p and its inverse-weighted readout is K modulo p. Thus the map's coordinate observation B_ρ(K)=G'(ρ)K mod p is linear on the fixed local chart. It correctly does not declare the nonlinear polynomial G a global additive observer. The separately generated ciphertext defect, coherent H input, source bound, noise and source-binding conditions remain implementation obligations.

**Recumbent quotient changes.** Changing the chosen remainder from r to r+d·a changes the quotient from q to q−a. This is exactly the affine reference-shift law. Dropping a modulus also changes representation capacity, so the checked fixed-map reference theorem alone is not a proof of the entire lane-drop API. The inspected Rust nearest-ties-positive route centers the remainder at the midpoint, consistent with this equation.

**Rational normalization.** For an integer numerator and positive denominator, gcd(|n|,d)>0. Dividing both by this GCD preserves the rational and yields a coprime pair; zero normalizes to (0,1). The map correctly leaves signed-pair semantics, canonical uniqueness and intermediate-capacity propagation to subsequent formalization. Pair representation does not require denominators to be units in every finite residue lane.

**Operator transport.** The commuting square T∘F_A=F_B∘T follows by evaluating both sides on E_A(x) and obtaining E_B(f(x)). It applies on the common admitted domain, with f mapping that domain into itself or with all partial-operation guards discharged. Faithful representations and mutually inverse transfers then give conjugacy on their valid images. History/schema preservation and heterogeneous operator meanings require their own stated maps; value preservation alone does not establish them.

**Vector/lattice targets.** For field-linear maps, kernel inclusion gives factorization on the observed image. For integer lattice observations with ambiguity N, the target Ck mod Γ is well-defined exactly when CN is contained in Γ. The map retains the separate obligations for normal forms, representative selection and preservation of a specified energy or involution form. Invertibility alone is not a unitary or skew-adjointness proof.

**Conservative interpolation.** Prefix flux C=U−V and height H=i−2U give H'=H+2C; applying the same linear interpolant preserves the equation at every real spatial coordinate. With |C|≤B, the shifted finite interval has 2B+1 integer candidates, so total period P>2B gives uniqueness even with overlapping moduli. A target modulo b dividing P needs no full interval selection. The map correctly treats the two snapshots as inputs and distinguishes this interpolation from an evolution law or PDE limit.

**Inverse limits.** Compatible stage operations commuting with reduction preserve the inverse-limit compatibility equations. A target modulus dividing a stage period is then consistent at all subsequent stages. The map correctly distinguishes a divisibility-cofinal family yielding the full profinite integers from products of distinct primes, and distinguishes either completion from the usual real continuum.
