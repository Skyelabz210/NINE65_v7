## Proof meaning and generalization

The new `FutureQuotient` content is substantive: equality of all future requested outputs yields an equivalence relation, actual quotient query/transition constructors, and the coarsest output-sufficient transition-stable relation. The additive specialization identifies the invariant invisible subgroup. The refinement fixed-point criterion has the claimed semantics. This does not yet formally refine Python's class numbering, prove its finite termination bound, or produce a compact quotient for every state space. A source subtype must still be closed under each declared total transition.

`AnchorValuations` proves the exact exponent rule and saturation recurrence, including minimality. The 180-declaration Std packet does not prove a Nat.gcd/prime-factorization bridge or the Rust/Python implementation's full semantics. The separate original-repository `AnchorSaturation` module now closes the Nat bridge with 13 compiled statements: positive seed/iteration, exact factorization recurrence, divisibility-minimal sufficient anchor, conservative D fuel, and correctness of stopping at any fixed point. All 13 statements and bodies were independently read. Factorization is used in the proof, not as an algorithm input; the D-fuel bound is a termination witness, not the implementation's performance claim. `ReflectionInvariant` correctly strengthens the AHOP involution and Descartes-form preservation to every commutative ring, including characteristic two and composite residue rings. Neither identity alone proves orbit hardness or cryptographic security.

Every new proof body was inspected. A fresh reviewer rebuild reused no project `.olean` file and reported only `Classical.choice`, `propext`, and `Quot.sound` where present. The author's admission/custom-axiom/false-closure/missing-audit negative controls were also inspected. No whole-architecture correctness claim follows from declaration counts.

The useful universal result is query factorization through authentic observation fibers, with transition-stable refinement when future operations matter. Familiar CRT or Garner algebra does not establish that the entire source-bound architecture, query interface, or domain construction is identical to prior work. Conversely, a new integration does not establish first mathematical priority for all its ingredients. The campaign appropriately preserves both distinctions; no exhaustive priority determination is made here.

## Runtime contracts

The clear residue path keeps only two live residues, public bounds and opaque local identity/epoch metadata. It derives winding only when needed. The safe API does not expose a raw-reading constructor or cloneable source authority. Independent tests confirm that equal residues from a different source do not authorize a plan, and an update invalidates its epoch even when the value is unchanged. Admission occurs before mutation; refusal retains the old valid state. Native self-squaring has the same checked-bound and epoch discipline.

The unrestricted planner and bounded execution model have intentionally different contracts. A pair modulo 36 and 37 cannot answer arbitrary unrestricted integer queries. A producer-certified interval `[0,1332)` selects one source, so the bounded runtime can answer arbitrary requested projections of that source. A `ProducerCandidate` is public planning metadata and cannot issue live authority. No contradiction arises from these separate criteria.

Adjacent quotient extraction is one modular subtraction at fixed word width. General quotient execution uses variable-time repeated subtraction, preflighted against a default public bound of 1,000,000 iterations. Independent boundary tests confirm that admission uses the public upper bound, including for actual source zero, and explicit budget changes are respected. Direct adjacent and modulus-one queries have zero loop budget. This route is a clear reference implementation, not a constant-time encrypted divider or an asymptotic speedup claim.

The opt-in compiler adapter obtains the real producer observation and plans every query before dispatching any. Serial and UNHAL/MANA paths preserve output order. Source review found no authority bypass or synthesized observation. Author-owned current evidence records 29 core tests and three caller tests in each serial/accelerated feature configuration; its source hashes match the reviewed files. These six caller tests are execution evidence, not encrypted computation or physical timing guarantees.

The encrypted GCD builder's fixed schedule is correctly masked at denominator zero. The 2w round bound is sufficient: within every two nonterminal rounds the positive second component falls below half its previous value. Independent actual DAG tests checked output, topological dependency order, `DisjointOr` exclusivity and internal output markers. The new direct table for widths at most four computes the same target directly from public candidate selectors, with no ciphertext-addressed lookup. Its public construction grows exponentially with width and is explicitly capped; it does not inherit a 2w encrypted-round description. Strategy guards reject unsupported widths before construction. Both strategies share existing key-domain and noise preflight; no noise history reset was introduced.

The rational-pair reference was read and its cross-cancellation proof checked. Normalized integer pairs retain denominator semantics even when a residue lane shares a factor with the denominator. The current scalar Python reference is not a residue-native or encrypted rational adapter. Its bounded input contract can refuse an oversized unreduced pair even if a later reduction would fit.

## Discovery proofs and essential counterexamples

The composite-module constructor is correct under verified unimodular `U,V` and diagonal `UBV`. Solving the diagonal scalar congruences gives direct target weights. A failed gcd divisibility condition gives an explicit source difference in the observation kernel but outside the target kernel. Rectangular matrices, modulus one, composite moduli and unused coordinates are included. This certifies query extraction; it does not implement a scalable Smith-form producer or create authentic observations.

The BBS result is an actual evolution intertwining, not merely interpolation of snapshots. For integer nearest-neighbor heights, the running maximum commutes with piecewise linear interpolation because an upward step below the current maximum cannot cross it in its interior. Hence `E(2M-H)=2 runningmax(EH)-EH`. Empty-left boundary and retaining or draining outgoing carrier are required. The model preserves carrier residues without a scalar carrier register. For `H=(0,-1,1)` at `t=5/4`, the two transforms differ: the nearest-neighbor premise cannot be removed. This is a continuous-space, discrete-time path theorem, not a PDE limit.

The Hasse-jet quotient formula follows from the coefficient product rule when `M=(t-a)^ν U` and `U(a)` is a unit. It extends the useful observation family when value-only observations erase the target. It needs authentic compatible jet readings and the vanishing/unit certificates. Ordinary derivatives fail as a substitute in small characteristic because factorials can vanish. Neither jet production nor encrypted refresh is supplied by the local readout formula alone.

The following independent adversarial witnesses remain part of acceptance:

* Sources 0 and 1332 have identical 36/37 observations but different full quotients. Capacity or query-specific sufficiency is necessary.
* A main reading from source 0 and anchor reading from source 1 gives `(0,1)`, which is arithmetically consistent with source 1296. Arithmetic consistency cannot establish common-source provenance.
* Moduli `[4,6,6]` provide period 12, not their product. For old period 12 and demanded period 18, an extra anchor 3 fails while 9 suffices.
* Parity identifies 0 and 2, but floor-halving separates their next outputs. Static query equivalence need not be a valid dynamic quotient.
* Sources -1 and 1331 share 36/37 observations but have opposite signs. A signed chart must be certified.
* The exact rational orthogonal rotation `[[3/5,-4/5],[4/5,3/5]]` maps `(1,1)` to `(-1/5,7/5)`: squared L2 remains two while L-infinity grows. Exact energy preservation alone does not establish a maximum principle or continuum regularity.

The corpus report preserves the older NS overclaim and the later blueprint's explicit open obligations. Its 181-record accounting distinguishes full reads, scoped excerpts, inherited reads, inventories and unavailable leads. The unavailable linked chat is not treated as read. The 231 pair/462 direction discovery screen is reproducible capability triage, not 231 verified breakthroughs. All twelve candidates retain assumptions and separate potential/readiness assessments.
