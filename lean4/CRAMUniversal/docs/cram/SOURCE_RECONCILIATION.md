## Torus-Fiber intake: reference coordinate is essential

Let pairwise-coprime carrier moduli have product M and let M_i=M/m_i. For normalized lane inputs let xi_i = x_i * inverse(M_i,m_i) mod m_i and S=sum(xi_i*M_i). Then the canonical representative x=S mod M satisfies S=x+alpha*M, with 0<=alpha<k for k lanes.

For positive anchor/check moduli A and q with gcd(M,A)=gcd(M,q)=1, and X=x+K*M, an anchor reading of **X** gives

    c=(X mod A - S mod A)*inverse(M,A) mod A = K-alpha mod A.

Consequently K=alpha+c mod A only when alpha is separately known, or the requested query is invariant under its unresolved choices. A witness reading of **x** gives

    alpha=(S mod q - x mod q)*inverse(M,q) mod q,

and q>=k recovers alpha exactly. Substituting X mod q gives alpha-K instead. This is a reference-change obligation, not a naming distinction. The universal transport theorem accommodates it, but does not supply a missing authentic witness.

The uploaded benchmarks generate canonical redundant readings using Garner during setup. This is acceptable as an oracle/fixture; the timing therefore measures a readout conditional on those readings already being available. Full S-alpha*M reconstruction is a declared output algorithm, not an automatically admitted residue hot path. The report's approximately 50.69x primitive and 6.32x full-output ratios are archived measurements on its ARM host, not universal constants or NINE65 integration results. The primitive itself does O(k) modular work, not O(1) in lane count.

Composite and prime-power examples support the parameterized Safe Basis contract: coprimality of the relevant factors, rather than primality or squarefreeness, enables the inverse. Dependent anchor/witness moduli may overlap each other when each meets its own relation to M. This does not establish that a Garner inverse table works on arbitrary noncoprime base lanes.

The fresh intake report records executable results, certificate-code gaps, exact hashes and which archived runs were reproduced. It is separate from the maintained Lean theorem inventory.
